1.1. Abstract
This document defines the canonical concept, brand identity, and minimum-viable-product
(MVP) scope for X Paradox X, the first title published under the X Dark X company banner in
the Dark Strategy (DS) genre. It supersedes, with explicit reconciliation, the earlier
ideation captured in chatGPT_records/chat_record_30072026.md, which used the working genre
label "eXtended Strategy Game (XSG)" and working title "X Paradox." This document is the
single source of truth for what the game is; all other files in game_specifications/ must
cite this document rather than restate or reinterpret its content.

1.2. Key Words
VoiceGate, voice agent, compliance testing, red-team, EU AI Act Article 50, California

Risk Report, LabLab AI Factory, Natively AI, hackathon, guardrails, prompt injection.

1.3. Executive Summary
VoiceGate is an automated compliance and safety test bench for production voice AI agents.
It calls the agent under test with simulated callers that vary by age, emotional state, and
adversarial style, drives them through a scenario gauntlet, transcribes both sides of every
call, and scores each call against two grounded references: the statutes that apply to the
deploying company (e.g. EU AI Act Article 50, California SB 243), and that company's own
published refund/cancellation terms. Every finding returns Pass, Fail, or Flag with the
specific rule or clause behind the verdict; a Fail produces a suggested system-prompt patch
that is re-run through the same gauntlet to prove the fix. This repository (X_Voice_X, a
py.typed Python package) was forked from the sibling DarkStrategy_XParadoxX project on
team-lead guidance (voice_transcription_concept/voice_chat_concept.md) specifically because
its documentation-first methodology — concept, then rules, then coding/testing standards,
then CICD/deployment/monitoring — transfers with only content-level changes, not
structural ones. The concept intentionally narrows initial scope to one working gauntlet run
end-to-end (Test Bench -> Call Review -> Risk Report) rather than broad but shallow coverage.

1.5. Philosophical Preamble
A concept document is the deed to a territory not yet built. Before a single line of Python
is written, before Natively AI generates a single component, the team must be able to answer,
without hesitation: what is this, who is it for, and what will we deliberately not build
first. VoiceGate's own subject matter — testing whether an AI agent tells the truth to a
caller in crisis — demands that its own specification corpus hold itself to the same
standard: no unverified claim asserted as fact, no speaker's words trusted as instructions,
no guardrail skipped because the deadline is a week away. Rigor here is not bureaucracy; it
is the same discipline the product exists to enforce on other people's voice agents.

1.10. MVP Scope Boundaries

1.10.1. In Scope
1.10.1.1. One end-to-end scenario-gauntlet run: launch from Test Bench, live transcript,
Call Review with inline verdicts, Risk Report with at least one before/after re-run.
1.10.1.2. A minimum viable persona/scenario catalog covering at least one entry each from the
Crisis/Emotional, Minor/Age-Sensitive, Adversarial-Refund, and Standard/Happy-Path categories
(see 025_scenario_persona_catalog.txt).
1.10.1.3. A minimum viable statutory ruleset corpus (EU AI Act Art. 50, CA SB 243, plus one
scraped example company terms page) with structured rule citations, not full statute text
(see 028_statutory_ruleset_integration.txt).
1.10.1.4. Dual-channel (caller/agent) transcription with role labels resistant to in-text
role-confusion and injected instructions.
1.10.1.5. Pass/Fail/Flag verdict scoring with a suggested-patch adviser (advisory-only, never
auto-applying a patch without human review).

1.10.2. Explicitly Out of Scope (MVP)
1.10.2.1. User accounts, authentication, billing, or multi-tenant isolation.
1.10.2.2. Live production call interception (MVP tests against a sandbox/staging instance of
the target voice agent only).
1.10.2.3. Full legal-advice generation; verdicts cite rule IDs and clause text, they do not
render legal opinions.
1.10.2.4. Automatic, unsupervised deployment of a suggested system-prompt patch to the
customer's live agent.
1.10.2.5. Items deferred to 018_voicegate_future_wishlist.txt are, by definition, out of MVP
scope.
