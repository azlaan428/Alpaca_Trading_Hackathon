1.1. Abstract
This document defines the canonical concept, brand identity, and minimum-viable-product
(MVP) scope for X Quant X, a multi-agent, cross-market quantitative intelligence
paper-trading platform. It is the single source of truth for what the platform is; all
other files in alpaca_paper_trading_specifications_x_quant_x/ must cite this document
rather than restate or reinterpret its content.

1.2. Key Words
X Quant X, quantitative finance, paper trading, multi-agent, Alpaca API, market regime,
operator model, four-domain, Earth-Air-Fire-Water, dashboard, P&L, LabLab, Python, Go,
C++, Rust.

1.3. Executive Summary
X Quant X is an automated quantitative paper-trading intelligence platform. It ingests live
and historical market data, infers market regimes using Bayesian and Kalman-filter state
estimation, aggregates signals from independent operator agents, gates them through a risk
rule-set, and places paper-trade orders via the Alpaca API. The system is partitioned into
four orthogonal views of one market: Earth (Portfolio & Income), Air (Hedging & Risk),
Fire (Volatility & Options), Water (Macro & Cross-Market). The platform is built in Python
(primary), Go, C++, and Rust. The MVP delivers: Paper trading via Alpaca paper API,
real-time P&L dashboard, risk-gate audit trail, regime probability display, and signal
attribution. The platform was developed as part of the LabLab hackathon on quantitative
AI-driven trading.

1.5. Philosophical Preamble
Documentation is a contract between agent and system: precision of structure mirrors
precision of thought. X Quant X extends that claim from documentation into the product
itself. A platform whose purpose is quantitative market intelligence forfeits its own
credibility the moment it accepts untested model outputs as ground truth, or asserts an
unverified return estimate as settled performance. Epistemic humility, risk discipline, and
evidence-traceability are therefore not bureaucratic overhead layered onto the platform;
they are the platform's core value proposition.

1.7. Elevator Pitch
X Quant X is a quantitative paper-trading platform that continuously determines the state
of financial markets, estimates uncertainty and risk, and dynamically proposes portfolio
allocation and trading decisions. Rather than relying on a single predictive model, it
composes typed Operators — DataOperator, MarketRegimeOperator, SignalOperator, RiskOperator,
PortfolioOperator, ExecutionOperator — into a DAG that processes market data into
paper-trade orders via the Alpaca API. The platform's central question: "Given all
information available at time t, what market regime is most probable, what risks dominate,
and what portfolio action provides the best risk-adjusted response?"

1.9. MVP Scope Boundaries

1.9.1. In Scope (MVP)
1.9.1.1. Paper trading via Alpaca paper API (alpaca-py SDK).
1.9.1.2. Real-time market data ingestion (Alpaca WebSocket).
1.9.1.3. Market regime inference (HMM + Bayesian + Kalman filter).
1.9.1.4. Signal operators: Momentum, MeanReversion, MacroTrend, Volatility.
1.9.1.5. Risk gate: 10 rules (CONC-001 through EXEC-001 per 028_xquantx_risk_ruleset.txt).
1.9.1.6. Portfolio operator: Mean-variance (R01/R11), Risk-parity (R03/R05),
Minimum-variance (R04/R07).
1.9.1.7. Dashboard: equity curve, drawdown, regime probability, positions, P&L, margins,
API call log, decisions log.
1.9.1.8. Backtest mode (historical OHLCV replay, optional).
1.9.1.9. Audit log (NDJSON, SHA-256 hash chain).
[1.9.1.10. Options can and MUST BE used in paper trading, not live execution as noted in 1.9.2. section]
1.9.2. Explicitly Out of Scope (MVP)
1.9.2.1. Live trading (real money).
1.9.2.2. Options execution.
1.9.2.3. Multi-broker support (Alpaca only).
1.9.2.4. Reinforcement-learning-based regime engine.
1.9.2.5. Real-time streaming LLM news pipeline (offline batch news scoring only).
1.9.2.6. Mobile dashboard (web/terminal only).
1.9.2.7. C# implementation (reference SDK only; not a primary language).

1.14. Conclusion
X Quant X is a quantitative paper-trading platform delivering a complete, auditable,
multi-agent pipeline from market data ingestion through risk-gated order execution to P&L
attribution. The MVP scope is intentionally bounded to paper trading to allow focused
development of the core quantitative intelligence pipeline before extending to live
execution.
