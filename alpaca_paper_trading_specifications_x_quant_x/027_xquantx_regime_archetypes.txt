1.1. Abstract
This document defines the Hidden Markov Model (HMM) state definitions, Bayesian prior
parameterization, and regime transition probability matrix conventions for each of the twelve
canonical market-regime archetypes (R01–R12) used by MarketRegimeOperator. It is the
authoritative parameterization reference for all regime inference implementations.

1.2. Key Words
regime archetype, Hidden Markov Model, HMM, Bayesian prior, transition matrix, regime
entropy, Kalman filter, MarketRegimeOperator, R01 through R12, bull-quiet, macro-shock,
regime dwell time, emission distribution.

1.3. Executive Summary
Each of the twelve market-regime archetypes (R01–R12 defined in
025_xquantx_scenario_catalog.txt) is parameterized as an HMM state with: (a) an emission
distribution over the feature space, (b) a Bayesian prior probability, (c) a row in the
regime transition matrix A, and (d) a minimum dwell time in bars before a regime transition
is considered stable. The transition matrix and priors are initialized from
config/regimes.toml and updated via Baum-Welch estimation on historical data.

1.4. Key Findings
1.4.1. Each regime is an HMM state with a multivariate Gaussian emission distribution over
the feature vector (RSI, MACD, ATR, VIX, volume ratio, cross-asset correlation, Hurst
exponent).
1.4.2. The transition matrix A (12x12) has the property that diagonal elements A_ii > 0.85
for all regimes — regimes are persistent; rapid switching between non-adjacent regimes
indicates noise, not true regime change.
1.4.3. Regime entropy H_t = -sum_k P(r_k|x_t) ln P(r_k|x_t) serves as an uncertainty gate:
when normalized entropy U_t > 0.5, downstream operators apply conservative sizing
regardless of signal scores.
1.4.4. Regime dwell time: a new regime label is confirmed only after >= MIN_REGIME_DWELL_BARS
(default 3) consecutive bars assign majority probability to the new regime.
1.4.5. Bayesian priors are calibrated on the historical dataset in config/regimes.toml; they
are updated quarterly per the maintenance policy in this document.

1.5. Philosophical Preamble
A regime archetype is not a prediction about the future; it is a structured description of a
recurring market condition. By parameterizing each archetype with an emission distribution
and a transition probability, the platform acknowledges that market regimes are latent, not
directly observable — and that honest quantification of transition uncertainty is more
valuable than confident mislabeling.

1.6. HMM State Definitions

1.6.1. R01 — bull-quiet
Emission: multivariate Gaussian mean mu_R01 = [RSI=62, MACD=+1, ATR=0.8s, VIX=13,
VolRatio=1.0, Corr=0.55, Hurst=0.52]. Covariance S_R01 is diagonal with low variances
(tight cluster). Prior P(R01) = 0.22 (most common peacetime regime). Transition:
A_R01->R01 = 0.92 (high persistence); most probable next regimes R02, R11.

1.6.2. R02 — bull-volatile
Emission: mu_R02 = [RSI=58, MACD=+0.5, ATR=1.3s, VIX=20, VolRatio=1.4, Corr=0.60,
Hurst=0.50]. Prior P(R02) = 0.15. A_R02->R02 = 0.87. Most probable transitions: R01, R05.

1.6.3. R03 — bear-trending
Emission: mu_R03 = [RSI=42, MACD=-0.7, ATR=1.4s, VIX=24, VolRatio=1.2, Corr=0.65,
Hurst=0.55]. Prior P(R03) = 0.12. A_R03->R03 = 0.88. Most probable transitions: R04, R05.

1.6.4. R04 — bear-capitulation
Emission: mu_R04 = [RSI=28, MACD=-2.0, ATR=2.5s, VIX=38, VolRatio=2.5, Corr=0.82,
Hurst=0.45]. Prior P(R04) = 0.04. A_R04->R04 = 0.72 (less persistent — capitulations are
acute). Most probable next: R03, R05, R01 (recovery). RiskOperator rule: all new long
positions BLOCKED (per 017_xquantx_scoring_rules.txt §1.7.6).

1.6.5. R05 — high-volatility
Emission: mu_R05 = [RSI=50, MACD=0.0, ATR=2.0s, VIX=28, VolRatio=1.6, Corr=0.70,
Hurst=0.48]. Prior P(R05) = 0.08. A_R05->R05 = 0.85. Most probable transitions: R03, R07.

1.6.6. R06 — low-liquidity
Emission: mu_R06 = [RSI=50, MACD=0.0, ATR=0.5s, VIX=14, VolRatio=0.3, Corr=0.50,
Hurst=0.50]. Prior P(R06) = 0.05. A_R06->R06 = 0.78. Seasonal/event-driven; typically
reverts to R01 or R02.

1.6.7. R07 — macro-shock
Emission: mu_R07 = [RSI=35, MACD=-1.5, ATR=3.0s, VIX=42, VolRatio=3.0, Corr=0.90,
Hurst=0.40]. Prior P(R07) = 0.03. A_R07->R07 = 0.60 (acute, short-lived). Most probable
next: R04, R05. RiskOperator rule: all new long positions BLOCKED (per
017_xquantx_scoring_rules.txt §1.7.6). Regime entropy typically very high during R07;
uncertainty gate activates.

1.6.8. R08 — correlation-breakdown
Emission: mu_R08 = [RSI=50, MACD=0.0, ATR=1.2s, VIX=20, VolRatio=1.0, Corr=0.20,
Hurst=0.50]. Prior P(R08) = 0.04. A_R08->R08 = 0.80. Idiosyncratic conditions; often
precedes sector-rotation.

1.6.9. R09 — sector-rotation
Emission: mu_R09 = [RSI=52, MACD=+0.2, ATR=1.0s, VIX=17, VolRatio=1.1, Corr=0.35,
Hurst=0.52]. Prior P(R09) = 0.06. A_R09->R09 = 0.82. Most probable transitions: R01, R08.

1.6.10. R10 — mean-reversion
Emission: mu_R10 = [RSI=50, MACD=0.0, ATR=0.9s, VIX=15, VolRatio=0.9, Corr=0.55,
Hurst=0.43]. Prior P(R10) = 0.07. A_R10->R10 = 0.86. Hurst < 0.45 is the discriminating
feature from R01.

1.6.11. R11 — momentum-continuation
Emission: mu_R11 = [RSI=65, MACD=+1.5, ATR=1.1s, VIX=14, VolRatio=1.3, Corr=0.60,
Hurst=0.58]. Prior P(R11) = 0.08. A_R11->R11 = 0.88. Hurst > 0.55 and positive
autocorrelation lag-1 are discriminating features.

1.6.12. R12 — rate-hike-cycle
Emission: mu_R12 = [RSI=48, MACD=-0.3, ATR=1.1s, VIX=18, VolRatio=1.0, Corr=0.60,
Hurst=0.50]. Prior P(R12) = 0.06. A_R12->R12 = 0.85. Discriminating feature: 2-year yield
rising > 25bp over past 60 bars AND 2s10s spread compressed.

1.7. Regime Transition Matrix A
The full 12x12 transition matrix A is declared in config/regimes.toml. Key properties:
1.7.1. All diagonal elements A_ii >= 0.60 (regime persistence guarantee).
1.7.2. Every row sums to 1.0 +/- 1e-9.
1.7.3. A is updated via Baum-Welch EM estimation on each quarterly historical dataset
refresh.

1.8. Regime Entropy Gate
Normalized entropy: U_t = H_t / ln(12) in [0, 1].
1.8.1. When U_t > 0.5: all signal weights are scaled by (1 - U_t) before aggregation
(reducing exposure during uncertain regime transitions).
1.8.2. When U_t > 0.75: RiskOperator applies a soft REDUCE to all proposed new positions.
1.8.3. When U_t > 0.90: RiskOperator BLOCKS all new positions until confidence recovers.

1.9. Regime Dwell Time
MIN_REGIME_DWELL_BARS = 3 (declared in config/regimes.toml). A regime transition from
R_old to R_new is confirmed only after R_new has held the highest posterior probability for
>= 3 consecutive bars. This prevents whipsaw transitions in high-entropy periods.
