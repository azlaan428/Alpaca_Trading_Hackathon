1.1. Abstract
This document defines how statutory and company-Terms-and-Conditions rules are integrated
into VoiceGate's compliance scoring without importing or reproducing full legal text. It
isolates what is cited, what is paraphrased, and what is explicitly excluded, repurposing the
sibling project's bounded-economy-integration pattern (import concept, adapt to system,
exclude the rest) for statutory-rule integration
(voice_processing_specifications/0000_documentation_standards.txt records this repurposing
lineage).

1.2. Key Words
statutory ruleset, rule citation, EU AI Act Article 50, California SB 243, company Terms and
Conditions, non-import boundary, verification-flagged, compliance scoring, VoiceGate.

1.3. Executive Summary
VoiceGate integrates statutory and contractual rules at the citation level: rule ID, section/
article number, and a short paraphrase, stored in `src/data/statutory_rules` and
`src/data/company_terms_snapshots`. It never reproduces full statute or T&C text verbatim in
code, logs, or documentation. Integration is constrained to a bounded citation layer that the
compliance scoring engine (`src/voicegate/compliance_scoring_engine`) and verdict engine
(`src/voicegate/verdict_engine`) consume to produce a Pass/Fail/Flag verdict with a rule
citation, never a restated legal opinion.

1.4. Key Findings
1.4.1. Rule citation (ID + section + paraphrase) is the primary imported concept; full
statutory text is never imported.
1.4.2. Company-terms integration mirrors rule citation in structure: a snapshot reference
plus paraphrase, not a verbatim reproduction of scraped terms beyond what is needed for a
specific verdict's rationale.
1.4.3. No statute is treated as confirmed law without a verification flag until independently
checked against its official source; this file inherits and does not relax that flag from
voice_processing_specifications/001_voice_transcription_concept.txt §1.9.
1.4.4. Integration is intentionally citation-only to keep VoiceGate legally defensible and to
avoid unauthorized reproduction of copyrighted or officially-published legal text.

1.5. Philosophical Preamble
Integration means citation, not reproduction. The goal is a verdict a human reviewer can
verify against the original rule text themselves, not a system that becomes an unlicensed,
potentially-inaccurate republisher of statutory or contractual language.

1.6. Scope and Authority
1.6.1. This file documents conceptual and citation-schema integration boundaries.
1.6.2. Authoritative statutory/scoring mechanic detail remains in
voice_processing_specifications/017_voicegate_scoring_rules.txt.
1.6.3. If a conflict exists,
voice_processing_specifications/017_voicegate_scoring_rules.txt is authoritative for scoring
mechanics; this file remains authoritative for the citation/non-reproduction boundary itself.

1.7. Imported Statutory/Contractual Concepts
1.7.1. Rule identity as a stable, referenceable key (`rule_id`).
1.7.2. Section/article/clause locator within the source rule.
1.7.3. A one-to-two-sentence paraphrase capturing the compliance-relevant obligation.
1.7.4. A verification-status flag distinguishing confirmed from citation-needed rules.

1.8. Rule Citation Schema
1.8.1. `rule_id` is a stable short code, e.g. `EU-AI-ACT-ART50`, `CA-SB243`.
1.8.2. `section_or_clause` locates the specific provision, e.g. "Article 50(1)" or "T&C
§4.2 Refund Window".
1.8.3. `paraphrase` is a short, non-verbatim summary of the obligation (e.g. "AI systems
must disclose to users that they are interacting with an AI system").
1.8.4. `verification_status` is one of `verification-flagged` (not yet independently
confirmed against the official source) or `confirmed` (independently checked).
1.8.5. Every verdict record
(voice_processing_specifications/021_voicegate_data_standards.txt §1.8.2) carrying a Fail or
Flag outcome must reference exactly one `rule_id` plus `section_or_clause`.

1.9. Explicit Non-Imports (Non-Reproduction Boundary)
1.9.1. Full statutory text (e.g. the complete text of EU AI Act Article 50 or California SB
243) is never reproduced verbatim in code, logs, documentation, or the Risk Report.
1.9.2. Full company Terms and Conditions text is never reproduced beyond the minimal clause
excerpt needed to justify a specific verdict's rationale, and even then only paraphrased
where feasible.
1.9.3. No legal advice or legal conclusion is asserted; every verdict is an automated,
citation-backed compliance signal for human review, not a legal determination.
1.9.4. EU AI Act Article 50 and California SB 243 remain `verification-flagged` (citation-
needed) per voice_processing_specifications/001_voice_transcription_concept.txt §1.9 until a
team member independently confirms the current statutory text against an official source.

1.10. Integration Points in the Scoring Loop
1.10.1. Compliance-scoring phase: the transcript is checked against the applicable rule
citations for the call's persona/scenario (e.g. minor-protection rules for a Minor persona
call, per voice_processing_specifications/027_persona_archetype_definitions.txt §1.10).
1.10.2. Verdict phase: a Pass/Fail/Flag outcome is emitted with its `rule_id` and
`section_or_clause`, per Section 1.8.5.
1.10.3. Patch-adviser phase: a Fail verdict may produce a suggested system-prompt patch
(`src/voicegate/patch_adviser`) referencing the same rule citation, never inventing a new
uncited obligation.

1.11. Company-Terms Snapshot Boundaries
1.11.1. Company-terms snapshots (`src/data/company_terms_snapshots`) are scraped, dated,
and versioned; a verdict must record which snapshot date it was scored against.
1.11.2. If the deploying company's brand/company name is not yet confirmed (per
voice_processing_specifications/001_voice_transcription_concept.txt §1.6.1), the
corresponding snapshot metadata field is marked TBD rather than populated with a guess.

1.12. Summary Table
| Topic | Integration Decision |
|------|-----------------------|
| Statutory rule identity | Imported as citation (rule_id + section + paraphrase) |
| Full statute text | Explicitly excluded, never reproduced verbatim |
| Company T&C identity | Imported as snapshot + citation, minimally excerpted |
| Legal conclusions | Explicitly excluded; verdicts are compliance signals, not legal advice |
| Verification status | EU AI Act Art. 50 and CA SB 243 remain verification-flagged |

1.14. Risk and Mitigation Table
| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| Accidental verbatim reproduction of statutory or T&C text | Medium | Critical | 1.9.1-1.9.2 binding non-reproduction rule; code/doc review must check for it |
| Verdict issued without a citation | Low | High | 1.8.5 makes `rule_id` + `section_or_clause` mandatory on Fail/Flag |

1.16. Conclusion
Statutory and contractual integration in VoiceGate is a bounded citation layer, not a
wholesale legal-text import, and this distinction is now explicit and enforceable across the
compliance scoring and verdict engines.
